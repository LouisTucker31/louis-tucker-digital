Bringing customer enquiries into one clear place

The business received enquiries through several different routes, including its website, email and telephone. Information was being copied between systems and tracked manually, which made it difficult to see what was new, what needed a response and what had already been dealt with.

The aim was to create a lightweight dashboard that gave the team one clear view of its enquiries without introducing a complicated new system.

The brief

Build a simple internal dashboard that brings enquiry information together, makes the current status clear and helps the team identify what needs attention next.

Scope

Enquiry dashboard Status tracking Search and filtering Priority indicators Enquiry details Summary reporting

Project image

Turning scattered enquiries into a clearer workflow

The first step was to identify the information the team actually needed when reviewing an enquiry. Rather than showing every available detail at once, I focused the main dashboard on the customer, enquiry type, date received, current status and next action.

Enquiries can be filtered by status, type or priority, making it easier to find the records that need attention. Each enquiry opens into a more detailed view where the team can review the information submitted and update its progress.

I also added a simple overview of enquiry activity so the team could see how many enquiries were new, in progress or completed without having to create a separate report.
