A clearer online presence for a growing local business

The business needed a clearer, more professional online presence that better reflected the quality of its services and gave potential customers confidence from the first visit. The existing site was dated, difficult to navigate and no longer represented the business as well as it should.

The brief

Create a simple, modern website that clearly presents the company’s services, works well across devices and gives potential customers a straightforward way to get in touch.

Scope

5-page website Responsive design Contact form Basic SEO CMS
Project image
Project image

Designed around how customers actually use the site

The focus was on making the website clearer, faster and easier to use, while keeping the design simple and appropriate for the business.

I simplified the structure and navigation so visitors could quickly understand the services on offer and move easily between the most important pages. Calls to action were made more prominent, helping users find contact information and enquiry options without unnecessary steps.

The site was built responsively across desktop, tablet and mobile, with performance and accessibility considered throughout. Content was organised into a clearer hierarchy, imagery was selected to support the design, and the CMS was configured so selected content could be updated easily after launch.
