A simple website that reflects the character of the business

The coffee shop needed a website that felt as welcoming and well considered as the space itself. Its existing online presence was limited and made it difficult for customers to find key information before visiting.

The brief

Create a simple, modern website that introduces the coffee shop, shows what customers can expect and makes practical information such as opening times, location and menus easy to find.

Scope

5-page website Responsive design Contact form Basic SEO CMS
Project image
Project image

Built around the information customers need most

The priority was to create a warm, straightforward site that gave customers useful information without making them search for it.

I kept the navigation simple and brought opening times, location, menu information and contact details closer to the top of the site. Calls to action were kept clear so visitors could quickly find directions, view the menu or get in touch.

The site was built for desktop, tablet and mobile, with photography used throughout to give people a better feel for the coffee shop before visiting. I also set up the CMS so the client could update selected content, such as opening times, menu information and images, without needing to edit the site itself.
