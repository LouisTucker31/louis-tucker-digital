Making it easier for customers to get an initial price

The business received regular enquiries from customers wanting a rough idea of cost before discussing a job. The same basic questions had to be answered repeatedly before enough information was available to give a useful estimate.

The aim was to turn that early part of the quoting process into a simple online tool, while still making it clear that the final price would depend on the details of the job.

The brief

Build a straightforward quote calculator that asks customers the right questions, adjusts the estimate based on their answers and gives them an easy way to send the details to the business afterwards.

Scope

Quote calculator Conditional questions Pricing logic Indicative estimates Mobile interface Enquiry handoff

Turning common quote questions into a simple online tool

The first step was to identify the information normally needed before the business could give a useful initial estimate. I turned those questions into a guided journey rather than presenting customers with one long form.

The calculator only shows questions that are relevant to the choices already made. Pricing rules then use those answers to calculate an indicative range, giving customers a useful starting point without presenting it as a guaranteed final quote.

At the end of the calculator, the customer can send their details and the information they have already entered to the business. This means the customer does not need to repeat themselves and the business receives a more useful enquiry from the start.

The interface was designed to work comfortably on mobile, with clear questions, simple controls and an obvious next step throughout.
