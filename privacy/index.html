Privacy policy

Last updated: [add date before publishing]. This page explains what happens to the information you submit through this site.

What this covers

This policy applies to information collected through the contact form on this website. It doesn't cover any other way you might get in touch, such as by phone or social media.

What's collected

When you use the contact form, I collect the name, email address, project type, and message you enter. No other data is collected automatically by this site: there's no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies beyond what's strictly needed to run the site.

How it's used

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry. They aren't added to a mailing list, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond answering the message you sent.

Who processes it

Form submissions are handled by Formspree, a third-party form processing service, which delivers your message to my email inbox. Formspree processes this data on my behalf under its own privacy policy, available at formspree.io.

How long it's kept

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to respond to you and, where a project goes ahead, for the length of our working relationship plus a reasonable period afterwards for business records. [Confirm your actual retention practice before publishing.]

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it's used. To do any of this, get in touch using the details below.
