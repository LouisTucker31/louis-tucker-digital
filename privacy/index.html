Louis Tucker Digital is a trading name used by Louis Tucker. I am responsible for the personal information collected through this website and when providing my services.

Information I collect

When you contact me or enquire about a project, I may collect your name, business name, email address, telephone number, website address and any information you provide about your project.

If you become a client, I may also hold project correspondence, files you provide, invoices and other information needed to deliver the agreed work.

The website or hosting provider may also process basic technical information, such as IP addresses and server logs, where necessary to operate and secure the website.

How I use your information

I use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, deliver agreed services, communicate with clients, maintain business records and operate the website securely.

The lawful bases I normally rely on are contract, or steps taken before entering into a contract, legitimate interests in running and promoting my business, and legal obligations where records must be retained.

Business-to-business contact

I may obtain professional contact information from publicly available business sources, such as company websites and business directories, where I believe my services may be relevant.

This may include your name, job title, business email address and organisation. I use this information on the basis of my legitimate interests in promoting Louis Tucker Digital.

You can object to receiving marketing from me at any time. If you ask me not to contact you again, I will respect that request.

The ICO specifically requires businesses using individuals' professional contact details for B2B marketing to provide privacy information, including where those details came from.

Sharing your information

I do not sell personal information.

I may share information with service providers where necessary to operate the business or deliver a project, such as website hosting, email, domain, payment, cloud-storage or accounting providers.

Where a provider processes information outside the UK, appropriate data-protection safeguards will be used where required.

How long I keep information

I keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed.

Enquiries that do not become projects will normally be deleted when they are no longer useful. Client, invoice and business records may be retained for longer where necessary for legal, tax or accounting purposes.

Cookies

Louis Tucker Digital does not currently use advertising or marketing cookies or cookie-based analytics.

The website may use technology that is strictly necessary for its operation or security. If I introduce non-essential cookies or tracking in future, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where consent is being relied upon.

To exercise your rights, email louistucker@live.co.uk.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are unhappy with how your information has been handled.

Changes to this notice

I may update this notice if the way I collect or use personal information changes. The latest version will always be available on this website.
