How the process works

You stay involved from the first conversation to launch.

01 Discovery

We talk through your business, your customers and what the website needs to achieve. I also confirm the pages, features, content and any extra services before work starts.

02 Design

I plan the structure, content hierarchy and visual direction. You see the initial approach before I build the full site, so changes can be made early.

03 Build and review

Once the direction is agreed, I build the full site with responsive design, performance, accessibility, security and basic SEO in mind. You then review it and request the agreed changes.

04 Launch

After approval, I complete the final checks, connect the domain and hosting where needed, and publish the site. I then make sure you have the information you need for handover.
