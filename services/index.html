How the process works

A straightforward process that keeps you involved at each stage, from the first conversation through to launch.

01 Discovery

We start with a short conversation about your business, your customers and what you want the website to achieve. I'll also confirm the pages, features, content and any additional services you may need before work begins.

02 Design

I plan the structure, layout, content hierarchy and visual direction around your business. You'll see the initial direction before the full website is completed, so any changes can be made early.

03 Build & review

Once the direction is agreed, I build the full website with responsive design, performance, accessibility, security and basic SEO in mind. You'll then review the site and request any agreed changes.

04 Launch

After final approval, I complete the final checks, configure the domain and hosting where needed, and get everything live. I'll also make sure you have the information you need once the project is handed over.
